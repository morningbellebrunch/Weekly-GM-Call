The Call

Tap a line to see what it asks for.

Ideas

One idea per person. Anything. Say it before we get into the numbers.

Week in Review

Make or miss on sales, labor, COGs, and reviews, and why. No figures read aloud. One win and one miss, named specifically.

Operations Focus

Top three things you are working to improve right now. What is moving, what is stuck. What can you use above store support on?

People

Top 20 percent and bottom 20 percent. Who gets recognized this week, who gets coached in, who needs coaching out.

Facilities

Anything down, deferred, or unsafe. What needs escalation above store.

Food and Bev

Questions, feedback, and needs. Out of stocks, execution issues, anything Wes needs to see.

Marketing and Events

Local events in the next 14 days. Food drops, Share It cards, catering outreach, orders on the books.

Before you join

One idea, one win, one miss, and your three. Bring the ask, not just the problem.
